DUAL-STREAM ARCHITECTURE · HUMAN–PET INTERACTION

人体—犬类骨架融合的人宠互动识别网络

人体与犬类骨架分别经过 ST-GCN 编码，结合跨主体关系特征与可选音频，在融合头中预测无互动、喂食、抚摸和共同玩耍。

ST-GCN backbones follow the same 64→128→256 channel progression as the single-dog model. Interaction-specific additions are the human stream, cross-subject relation vector and late-fusion classifier.
