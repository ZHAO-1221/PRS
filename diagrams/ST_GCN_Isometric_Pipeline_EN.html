Dog-Skeleton ST-GCN: From Keypoint Sequences to Behavior Probabilities

Original horizontal architecture; isometric feature slabs show channel expansion and temporal downsampling without reusing the reference layout, color coding, or visual assets.

Architecture basis: Yan, Xiong & Lin, AAAI 2018. Visual composition, dog-skeleton glyph, feature-block geometry and colour system are newly drawn for this project.
