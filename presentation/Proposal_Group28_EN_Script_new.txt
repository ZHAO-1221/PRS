[EN] Hello, everyone. We are Group 28. I'm Sun Wenjing, and my teammate is Zhao Jiayi. Our project recognizes indoor dog behavior and human–dog interaction from video, using skeleton features. The idea is simple: a home camera should not just see the dog. It should understand what the dog is doing, and how people interact with it. On the right is our mobile prototype. It turns camera video into a short daily report, showing key moments and how long the dog rested, played, or spent time with people. I will cover the background, goals, system and data. Then Jiayi will present the models and evaluation.

[中文] 各位老师、同学早上好，我们是第 28 组。我是 Sun Wenjing，这位是我的组员 Zhao Jiayi。我们的项目是基于视频和骨架特征，识别室内狗的行为和人宠互动。想法很简单：家里的摄像头不应该只是看到狗，还应该看懂狗在做什么、人和狗怎样互动。右边是我们的手机端原型：它把摄像头视频变成一份简短的每日报告，列出关键片段，以及狗休息、活动、和人在一起各花了多少时间。我先介绍背景、目标、系统和数据，然后由 Jiayi 介绍模型和评估。


【Slide 2 / 12 · 第 2 页】 Speaker 主讲: Sun Wenjing · 0:50–2:15 (~1.4 min)

[EN] First Why this project? Many families have pets today, and many owners see their dog as part of the family. When they are away, they want to know if the dog is safe and healthy. The market reflects this. The global pet camera market was about 198 million US dollars in 2024, and it may reach 454 million by 2030, growing about 12.6 percent a year.

But most pet cameras can only see and track. They find the dog, follow it, and send an alert when there is motion or noise. Owners want to know more: Did my dog eat? Did someone play with it? Right now, they still have to watch the videos by themselves.

Also, a single frame is not enough. In one picture, eating looks almost the same as sniffing, and petting looks like just standing nearby. The real difference is how the body moves over time, and how the person and the dog move together. So our project adds one step: from seeing to understanding pets behavior.

[中文] 为什么做这个项目？现在很多家庭养宠物，很多主人把狗当成家人。不在家时，他们想知道狗是否安全、健康。市场也反映了这一点：2024 年全球宠物摄像头市场约 1.98 亿美元，到 2030 年可能达到 4.54 亿，每年增长约 12.6%。
但大多数宠物摄像头只能“看见”：找到狗、跟着拍、有动静或声音就提醒。主人想知道更多：狗吃饭了吗？有人陪它玩吗？现在他们还是得自己翻录像。
而且只看一帧是不够的。单看一张图，进食和闻嗅几乎一样，抚摸和只是站在旁边也很像。真正的区别在于身体随时间怎么动，以及人和狗怎么一起动。所以我们的项目要补上一步：从“看得见”到“看得懂”。


【Slide 3 / 12 · 第 3 页】 Speaker 主讲: Sun Wenjing · 2:15–3:20 (~1.1 min)

[EN] Our goal is a working app. The user uploads an indoor video, and the system reports three things: what the dog is doing, how the person and the dog interact, and when it happens, each with a confidence score.

We have two tasks. Task A is dog behavior recognition. It looks only at the dog, using the dog skeleton over time. We use ST-GCN, and there are six classes. Task B is human–dog interaction recognition. It looks at both the person and the dog, using two skeletons plus relation features, and audio is optional. We use a two-stream ST-GCN, and there are four classes. One clip can have both labels. For example, the dog is eating while the person is feeding it.

[中文] 我们的目标是做一个能运行的原型。用户上传一段室内视频，系统给出三件事：狗在做什么、人和狗怎样互动、发生在什么时间，并附上置信度。
我们有两个任务。左边是 Task A，犬行为识别，只看狗，输入是狗骨架随时间的变化，用 ST-GCN，一共 6 类。右边是 Task B，人宠互动识别，同时看人和狗，输入是两个骨架加关系特征，音频可选，用双流 ST-GCN，一共 4 类。一个片段可以同时有两个标签，比如狗在吃东西，同时人在喂它。


【Slide 4 / 12 · 第 4 页】 Speaker 主讲: Sun Wenjing · 3:20–4:45 (~1.4 min)

[EN] This is the full system.
The top half is offline training. The dog pose model starts from a pretrained model, then trains on dog images from APT-36K, and finally on our own indoor data. For human pose, we use a ready-made pretrained model. For the recognition models, we choose the best one on the validation set, and we use the test set only once at the end.

The bottom half is online inference, in six steps. Step One, we take an indoor video. Step Two, we detect and track every person and dog, so each one keeps the same ID. Step Three, we find their body joints. Step Four, we turn the joints into skeleton graphs. Step Five, we recognize the behavior and the interaction. Step Six, we output the class, the confidence and the time.

The key idea is simple: the skeleton connects everything. Pose estimation finds the joints, and ST-GCN understands the movement.

[中文] 这是整个系统，后面每一页都是放大其中一部分。
上半部分是离线训练。狗姿态模型从一个预训练模型出发，先用 APT-36K 的狗图片训练，再用我们自己的室内数据训练。人体姿态直接用现成的预训练模型。识别模型用验证集选出最好的，测试集只在最后用一次。
下半部分是在线推理，一共六步：一，输入室内视频；二，检测并跟踪每个人和每只狗，让每个目标保持同一个 ID；三，找出身体关节点；四，把关节点变成骨架图；五，识别行为和互动；六，输出类别、置信度和时间。
核心思想很简单：骨架把一切连接起来。姿态估计负责找关节，ST-GCN 负责理解动作。



【Slide 5 / 12 · 第 5 页】 Speaker 主讲: Sun Wenjing · 4:45–6:15 (~1.5 min)

[EN] Now, where does our data come from? There are two sources.

The first is a public dataset, APT-36K. We use only its dog images, keep the official split, and do not relabel them. Why not AP-10K? Because our pose model was already trained on it. Training on it again teaches the model nothing new, and the test would not be fair.

The second source is indoor videos, either used with permission or publicly available. Here we follow one key rule. We split the data by dog and by video first, and only then cut frames and clips. So the same dog or the same video never appears in both training and testing. This prevents data leakage.

In the end, we get three kinds of data. First, 300 to 800 indoor images, each labeled with a dog box and 17 joints. Second, behavior and interaction labels for each clip. Third, features we extract automatically, such as human joints, objects and sound.

[中文] 我们的数据从哪里来？有两个来源。
第一个是公开数据集 APT-36K。我们只用其中狗的图片，沿用官方划分，不重新标注。为什么不用 AP-10K？因为我们的姿态模型已经在它上面训练过，再训练一次学不到新东西，测试也不公平。
第二个来源是室内视频，有的获得了授权，有的是公开视频。这里我们遵守一条关键规则：先按狗和视频划分数据，再抽帧、切片段。这样同一只狗、同一段视频不会同时出现在训练和测试里，避免数据泄漏。
最后我们得到三类数据：第一，300 到 800 张室内图片，每张标出狗框和 17 个关节点；第二，每个片段的行为和互动标签；第三，自动提取的特征，比如人体关节、物体和声音。


【Slide 6 / 12 · 第 6 页】 Speaker 主讲: Sun Wenjing · 6:15–7:30 (~1.25 min) → hand over

[EN] Next, we train the dog pose model in three stages.

P0 is the baseline. It is the official pretrained model with no extra training, tested directly on our indoor data. This shows how it handles hard cases, like occlusion or low light.

P1 starts from P0 and trains on the APT-36K dog images, to better identify dogs and find their skeleton.

P2 starts from P1 and fine-tunes on our own indoor images, with a small learning rate and data augmentation.

We test all three on the same test set, using standard keypoint metrics, AP and PCK. If P1 beats P0, dog data helps. If P2 beats P1, indoor data helps.

That's all for the data part. Now Jiayi will explain the models.

[中文] 接下来，狗姿态模型分三个阶段训练。
P0 是基线：官方预训练模型，不做额外训练，直接在我们的室内数据上测试，看它在遮挡、光线暗这些难的情况下表现如何。
P1 从 P0 出发，用 APT-36K 的狗图片训练，让模型更准地找到头和腿。
P2 从 P1 出发，用我们自己的室内图片微调，学习率较小，并加上数据增强。
三个版本在同一个测试集上比较，用标准的关键点指标 AP 和 PCK。如果 P1 比 P0 好，说明狗的数据有用；如果 P2 比 P1 好，说明室内数据有用。
数据部分就到这里，下面请 Jiayi 介绍模型。

【Slide 7 / 12 · 第 7 页】 Speaker 主讲: Zhao Jiayi · 7:30–8:50 (~1.3 min)

[EN] Thank you, Wenjing. Now let's look at the online part. This slide is step three: finding the joints of the person and the dog. We have two branches, one for the person and one for the dog. Both use the same design, called RTMPose. First, we cut out the person or the dog from the frame. Next, a backbone called CSPNeXt-M turns the image into features. It outputs 768 channels. Then a head called RTMCCHead processes these features. Finally, SimCC finds the x and y position of each joint. It treats this as a classification problem. The two branches have one difference: the input size. The person image is 256 by 192. The dog image is 256 by 256. They also do not share weights, because people and dogs have very different bodies. Each branch finds 17 joints in every frame. Each joint has an x value, a y value and a confidence score. Then we link the joints across frames by track ID. We also fill in missing joints and smooth the result. The final output has two streams, T frames, 17 joints and 3 values.

[中文] 谢谢 Wenjing。下面看在线部分。这一页是第三步：找出人和狗的关节点。我们有两个分支，一个给人，一个给狗，结构相同，都是 RTMPose。首先，从画面里把人或狗裁剪出来。然后，主干网络 CSPNeXt-M 把图片变成特征，输出 768 个通道。接着，RTMCCHead 处理这些特征。最后，SimCC 找出每个关节的 x 和 y 位置，它把这个问题当作分类来做。两个分支只有一个区别：输入尺寸。人的图片是 256×192，狗的图片是 256×256。两个分支也不共享权重，因为人和狗的身体差别很大。每个分支在每一帧找出 17 个关节点，每个点有 x、y 和置信度。然后按跟踪 ID 把各帧的关节连起来，补上缺失的点，再做平滑。最终输出是 2 条流、T 帧、17 个关节、3 个值。


【Slide 8 / 12 · 第 8 页】 Speaker 主讲: Zhao Jiayi · 8:50–10:05 (~1.25 min)

[EN] Now we have skeletons. Next, we use graph convolution to recognize actions. This slide shows how graph convolution works on a skeleton. This picture is an example, not a pipeline. In the top row, the center joint is the right elbow of a person. In the bottom row, it is the left elbow of a dog. The dashed circle shows its direct neighbors. The ST-GCN paper gives three ways to group these neighbors. The first way puts all of them in one group. The second way uses two groups: the joint itself, and its neighbors. The third way uses three groups, based on the center of the body. One group is the joint itself. One group is closer to the center. One group is farther from the center. We use the third way in both of our models. Each group has its own matrix. We call them A0, A1 and A2. You will see them on the next slide.

[中文] 现在有了骨架，下一步用图卷积来识别动作。这一页说明图卷积在骨架上是怎么做的。这张图是一个例子，不是流程图。上面一行，中心关节是人的右肘；下面一行，中心关节是狗的左肘。虚线圈出的是它的直接邻居。ST-GCN 论文给了三种分组方法。第一种，把所有邻居放在一组。第二种，分两组：关节自己一组，邻居一组。第三种，按身体重心分三组：关节自己一组，离重心更近的一组，离重心更远的一组。我们两个模型都用第三种。每一组有自己的矩阵，叫 A0、A1 和 A2，下一页会看到。


【Slide 9 / 12 · 第 9 页】 Speaker 主讲: Zhao Jiayi · 10:05–11:25 (~1.3 min)

[EN] This is Task A, the dog behavior model. We follow the original ST-GCN paper by Yan and others, from AAAI 2018. The input is the dog skeleton. It has T frames, 17 joints and 3 values. First, we encode the graph and normalize the data. Then we have nine ST-GCN blocks in three stages. Stage one has 64 channels. Stage two has 128 channels, and the time length becomes half. Stage three has 256 channels, and the time length becomes a quarter. Each block does two things. First, it looks at nearby joints in the same frame, using A0 to A2. Then, it looks at the same joint across 9 frames. A shortcut adds the input back. After the blocks, we average all the features into one vector of 256 numbers. A linear layer and softmax then give the probability of each of the six behaviors. Dogs lie down much more than they play, so our data is not balanced. To fix this, we use a weighted cross-entropy loss. We compare this model with an LSTM.

[中文] 这是 Task A，犬行为识别模型，沿用 Yan 等人 AAAI 2018 的 ST-GCN 原论文。输入是狗的骨架：T 帧、17 个关节、3 个值。先做图编码和归一化，然后是九个 ST-GCN 模块，分三个阶段。第一阶段 64 通道；第二阶段 128 通道，时间长度减半；第三阶段 256 通道，时间长度变成四分之一。每个模块做两件事：先看同一帧里相邻的关节，用 A0 到 A2；再看同一个关节在 9 帧里的变化。最后用残差连接把输入加回来。所有模块之后，把特征平均成一个 256 维的向量，再经过线性层和 softmax，得到六种行为的概率。狗躺着的时间比玩的时间多很多，所以数据不平衡，我们用加权交叉熵来解决。对比模型是 LSTM。


【Slide 10 / 12 · 第 10 页】 Speaker 主讲: Zhao Jiayi · 11:25–12:55 (~1.5 min)

[EN] Task A only looks at the dog. Task B also looks at the person. It recognizes how they interact. We use two streams. The human skeleton goes into one ST-GCN. The dog skeleton goes into another ST-GCN. Each stream has the same three stages: 64, 128 and 256 channels. We get a human feature, f_h, and a dog feature, f_d. But the two skeletons are not enough. Feeding, petting and playing are different mainly because of how the person and the dog move together. So we add relation features, f_r. They include the distance between them, their speed toward each other, and which way they face. We can also add sound features from YAMNet or MERT. This is optional. At the end, we join all the features together. Then a small MLP gives the result. This is called late fusion. There are four classes: no interaction, feeding, petting and playing together. Late fusion has one big benefit: sound is optional. If a video has no sound, or bad sound, the model still works with skeletons only. This is important for real home cameras.

[中文] Task A 只看狗，Task B 还要看人，识别人和狗怎样互动。我们用两条流：人骨架进一个 ST-GCN，狗骨架进另一个 ST-GCN。每条流都有同样的三个阶段：64、128、256 通道，分别得到人的特征 f_h 和狗的特征 f_d。但只有两个骨架还不够，因为喂食、抚摸和玩耍的区别，主要在于人和狗怎么一起动。所以我们加入关系特征 f_r，包括两者的距离、相互靠近的速度和朝向。我们还可以加入声音特征，用 YAMNet 或 MERT 提取，这是可选的。最后把所有特征拼在一起，用一个小的 MLP 给出结果，这叫后期融合。一共 4 类：无互动、喂食、抚摸、一起玩。后期融合有一个很大的好处：声音是可选的。如果视频没有声音或声音很差，模型只用骨架也能工作。这对真实的家用摄像头很重要。


【Slide 11 / 12 · 第 11 页】 Speaker 主讲: Zhao Jiayi · 12:55–14:20 (~1.4 min)

[EN] Finally, how do we show that each step really helps? We test four things. First, pose estimation. We compare P0, P1 and P2 with COCO AP, AP at 0.5, AP at 0.75 and PCK. We also report the head, front legs and back legs separately. Second, dog behavior. We report accuracy, macro-F1 and the confusion matrix. We compare LSTM with ST-GCN. We also remove some parts one at a time, to see if they help. These parts are the confidence score, the way we fill in missing joints, and data augmentation. Third, interaction. We use the same scores. We run three tests: one stream or two streams, with or without relation features, and with or without sound. Fourth, robustness. Our test set uses new dogs and new videos. So it shows how the model works on dogs it has never seen. We also check hard cases: hidden body parts, missing joints, many people or dogs, and no sound. We follow one rule the whole time. The validation set is only for choosing models. The test set is used only once, at the end.

[中文] 最后，怎么证明每一步真的有用？我们测四个方面。第一，姿态估计：用 COCO AP、AP@0.5、AP@0.75 和 PCK 比较 P0、P1、P2，并分别报告头部、前腿和后腿。第二，犬行为：报告准确率、macro-F1 和混淆矩阵，比较 LSTM 和 ST-GCN；还会一次去掉一个部分，看它有没有用，包括置信度、补缺失关节的方法和数据增强。第三，人宠互动：用同样的指标，做三组对比：单流还是双流、有没有关系特征、有没有声音。第四，鲁棒性：测试集用的是新的狗和新的视频，所以能看出模型在没见过的狗身上表现如何；我们还会检查难的情况：身体被遮挡、关节缺失、多人多狗、没有声音。整个过程遵守一条规则：验证集只用来选模型，测试集只在最后用一次。
