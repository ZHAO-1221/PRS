## Approved design language

- Primary metaphor: a dog’s detected day becomes a collection of playable story stickers.
- Pet-specific elements: paw-stamp time markers, sticker-like clip cards, restrained pet patterns, and friendly rounded forms.
- Information requirements retained: explicit timestamps, dog activity versus human–dog interaction, clip playback, analysis duration, and summary values.
- Tone: warm, lively, memorable, and caring; not childish, clinical, or e-commerce-like.
- Platform: iOS, using the standard iPhone frame and iOS-safe interaction patterns.
- Language: English only.
- Product naming: no final product name or logo yet; functional labels remain provisional.

## Iteration rule

Subsequent screens, components, color refinements, and interactions that remain within the Sticker Story language are treated as iterations of this approved direction and do not require a new three-direction selection round.
